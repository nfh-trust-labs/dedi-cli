# Code of Conduct

**Scope:** All participation spaces in this repository — Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and any other collaboration channel associated with `dedi-cli`.

---

## Purpose

`dedi-cli` handles private key material and implements part of the DeDi
protocol's trust model. The standards of conduct here favor collaboration
that is direct, evidence-based, and respectful — so that technical decisions
are made on their merits and contributors can do their work without pressure
or bad-faith argumentation.

---

## Expected behavior

- Communicate in clear, precise, and constructive terms.
- Ground technical claims in evidence — reproducible test cases or documented
  behavior — rather than assertion.
- Accept maintainer review decisions, even when the outcome is unfavorable,
  and raise disagreement through further discussion rather than repetition.
- Treat every contributor's time as a finite and valuable resource.

---

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, harassment, intimidation, or abusive language directed
  at any participant.
- Repeated argumentation on a decision that has already been made and
  documented by a maintainer.
- Pressuring maintainers to force a merge, accelerate a review, or bypass
  stated project conventions (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Misrepresenting the review or approval status of a change.

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers may close, lock, or edit issues, pull requests, and comments
that violate this Code of Conduct, and may restrict or remove repeat or
severe offenders from participating in the repository.

To report a concern, open an issue, or, if it involves another person
directly, contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile.

---

## Applicability

This Code of Conduct applies to all participation in spaces associated with
this repository, including Issues, Pull Requests, and Discussions. No
participant is exempt.
